### Architecture & Philosophy

Officially accepted and featured in the upstream MangoWM documentation showcase. Engineered for ultra-low latency, keyboard-driven navigation, and zero unnecessary background processes under Wayland. Integrates dynamic tiling logic, custom Waybar status monitors, and synchronized Catppuccin Mocha color tokens.
