### Architecture & Philosophy

Engineered for ultra-low latency, keyboard-driven navigation, and zero unnecessary background processes under Wayland. Integrates dynamic tiling logic, custom Waybar status monitors, and synchronized Catppuccin Mocha color tokens. Includes a publicly deployed web showcase displaying live layout mechanics and workflow aesthetics.
